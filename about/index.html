About

I’m Utkarsh. I grew up in Silicon Valley and study data science and applied math at UC Berkeley, class of 2028.

Background

I was born in Mountain View and grew up around Mountain View, Cupertino and Los Altos, surrounded by people building technology. I went to Saint Francis High School, where most of my time went into distance running.

I started college at UW–Madison in data science and economics and worked with faculty and PhD researchers on campus energy use. In 2025 I came back to the Bay Area as a transfer to Berkeley, one of about 40 out-of-state transfer admits a year.

Most of what I build sits where data meets the physical world: power grids, batteries, data centers, engines. I like projects that keep running after I stop typing, and numbers I can check against the source.

Running

Running was my life in high school. I captained the varsity team at Saint Francis, qualified for the CIF California state cross country championships, and won my age group at the 2022 San Francisco Half Marathon at 17. At my peak I was running about 50 miles a week.

India and Project Rishi

I’ve been to India about 14 times, mostly to see my grandparents in Delhi, and seeing how differently schools there are resourced stayed with me. At Berkeley I joined Project Rishi’s education team in my first semester and became Director of Internal Development in 2026. I now lead a team of about 10 working on a coaching center in a rural village, including the fundraising and budget. I’m also taking Hindi at Berkeley.

Cars

I’m into how cars work under the skin: engine software, control modules, infotainment, driver assistance and EV drivetrains. I reprogrammed my BMW’s engine computer myself, adjusting 125 calibration parameters, and added live G-force telemetry and radar displays to its screens. More on the projects page.
