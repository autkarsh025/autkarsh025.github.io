About

I grew up in Mountain View. In high school I was a distance runner: varsity captain at Saint Francis, a CIF California state cross country qualifier, and the age-group winner at the 2022 San Francisco Half Marathon when I was 17.

I started college at Wisconsin in data science and economics, then transferred to Berkeley, which takes about 40 out-of-state transfers a year. I study data science and applied math, and I am a project lead at Project Rishi, a 40-person group building an education center in rural India.

The projects on this site run every morning on their own. When I’m not working on them I’m usually reprogramming a car.
