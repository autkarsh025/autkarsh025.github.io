Gigawatt Ledger

AI infrastructure, from capital spending to the power grid

Follows the AI build-out through 26 companies' SEC filings (cloud buyers, chips, servers, power equipment, data-center landlords and utilities) and through hourly electricity demand in five data-center regions, with weather removed. Every number is stored with the date it became public, so history can be replayed without hindsight. An AI analyst answers questions about the data, and code checks every figure it writes.

Sundown

How batteries cover the evening peak on U.S. power grids

An automated pipeline that collects hourly grid data for the seven major U.S. power grids every morning, checks it, stores it and measures how much grid-scale batteries help after sunset, when solar disappears and demand peaks. It also tracks how clean the grid is hour by hour, when an electric car is cleanest to charge, and which readings look abnormal.

  • 25.7%of California's evening peak supplied by batteries, last 30 days
  • 7U.S. power grids, hourly, updated every morning
  • 586,000hourly records validated and stored
