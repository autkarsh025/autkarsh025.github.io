Projects

Gigawatt Ledger

Tracks AI spending from big tech to the power grid

A daily pipeline joins SEC filings from 26 companies across the AI supply chain with hourly electricity demand in five data-center regions, weather removed. Every number keeps the date it became public, so any past day can be replayed without hindsight. An AI analyst answers questions from 4,600+ filing passages, and code checks every figure it writes; it passes all 22 cases in its eval suite, prompt injection included.

Sundown

How batteries carry U.S. grids through the evening peak

Every morning a pipeline pulls hourly data for the seven major U.S. grids into SQL with Airflow and Spark, checks it, and rebuilds a live dashboard. Batteries now cover about a quarter of California's evening peak. The quality checks and 59 tests caught 3 errors in the government's own source data, and the carbon analysis shows midday EV charging in California emits about 60% less CO₂ than overnight.
